Questions

Who counts as a user?

A person in the team. AI agents, servers and other machines are devices, not users. A Teams license includes {{P:devices_per_user}} devices per user.

What if we go over the limit?

Without a license, a team server serves up to {{P:free_users}} people and {{P:free_devices}} devices over all its teams; a license raises that to its users, with {{P:devices_per_user}} devices each. Over the limit, the server refuses to add more people or devices, and says so. Nobody already in a team is removed or locked out, and removing people always works. If you need more devices than your license includes, or have more than {{P:enterprise_users}} users, write to us.

How does renewal work?

A license is not renewed without your consent. The license file has an end date; to renew, you get a new file.

Does the license check in with you?

No. The license file is checked offline, and your server never calls us to check it.

Is there a hosted version?

No. RemoHop Teams is self-hosted only: the team server runs inside your own rendezvous server, remohop signal.

Can we use the free edition at work?

Yes. The open-source edition is free for any use, commercial use included, and it never checks how it is used. If you modify the server and run it for other people, the AGPL asks you to offer them your changes; see Licensing.
