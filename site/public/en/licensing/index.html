Commercial license

If the AGPL-3.0 does not fit your use, a commercial license is available from the copyright holder, Readori. Typical cases:

  • shipping RemoHop inside a closed-source product;
  • running a modified server for others without publishing your changes;
  • distributing it through a store whose terms conflict with the AGPL.

For commercial licensing, write to support@readori.com. Say how you plan to use RemoHop (embedded in a product, as a hosted service, or distributed internally) and at roughly what scale.

Contributions

Because RemoHop is also offered under a commercial license, a contribution must be covered by a signed Contributor License Agreement (CLA) that lets the copyright holder distribute it under both licenses. You keep the copyright in your work. See CONTRIBUTING.md in the repository; questions about the CLA can go to support@readori.com.

Third-party software

RemoHop links Go modules under the MIT, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC and Apache-2.0 licenses, all compatible with the AGPL-3.0. Release archives will include their license texts as THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt.

RemoHop does not include or distribute ffmpeg. The remote desktop runs the ffmpeg already installed on the host as a separate program. Anyone who bundles an ffmpeg build with RemoHop takes on that build's own license terms: ffmpeg with libx264 is under the GPL.

The VPN client on Windows needs the Wintun driver, which remohop neither includes nor downloads.
